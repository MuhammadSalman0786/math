How Proportion & Cross-Multiplication Works

Solving Unknown Variables in Rational Fractions \( rac{A}{B} = rac{C}{D}\)

A mathematical proportion is an equation stating that two distinct ratios or fractions are equivalent. In standard form, given \( rac{A}{B} = rac{C}{D}\), the fundamental property of cross-products dictates that the product of extremes equals the product of means: \(A imes D = B imes C\).

Our Proportion Calculator allows you to place an unknown variable (such as \(x\)) in any of the four numerator or denominator fields. It instantly generates the isolated cross product, performs right-hand numeric multiplication, isolates the variable through division, and yields accurate 4-decimal precision.

AC to DC Conversion & Rectification Fundamentals

Peak Voltage, Root Mean Square (RMS), & DC Output Characteristics

Alternating Current (AC) oscillates periodically in a sinusoidal waveform. For a Full-Wave Bridge Rectifier, both polarities of the AC cycle are directed to the load, resulting in an average DC voltage of \(V_{DC} = rac{2 imes V_{peak}}{\pi} pprox 0.6366 imes V_{peak}\). In contrast, a Half-Wave Rectifier utilizes only one half of the waveform, delivering \(V_{DC} = rac{V_{peak}}{\pi} pprox 0.3183 imes V_{peak}\).
